Root–third overtone beat frequency.

The display models the interaction between the root's 5th (major) or 6th (minor) harmonic and the third's 4th (major) or 5th (minor) harmonic. At a pure 5:4 third, they coincide. The graph is a slowed-down envelope visualization, not an oscilloscope recording.

Scoring: Levels 1–3 use 100 × exp(−RMS error / 10 cents). Level 4 awards 25 points for the correct note, 25 for the correct direction, and up to 50 for tuning accuracy. The root stays fixed, and any inactive note is automatically set to its pure target. In progressive mode, two consecutive scores of at least 85/100 unlock the next detuning range. The 10-chord mode uses the tuning score for each note, averaged over ten chords. Progress lasts for the current page session.
